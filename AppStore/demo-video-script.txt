INSTABLOCK 1.0 – APP REVIEW DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT (physical iPhone, ~90 s)

Prep: delete and reinstall InstaBlock; Instagram signed in; Do Not Disturb on;
record via QuickTime (iPhone over USB, File > New Movie Recording) or iOS screen recorder.
Captions, no voice-over.

1. FIRST RUN (0:00–0:20)
   Launch InstaBlock. Explainer "One quick permission" -> Continue.
   Allow notifications. Allow "Add VPN Configurations".
   Hold on main screen: "Instagram and TikTok videos are blocked", VPN badge visible.
   Caption: "Local filter only – no server, no account, no data collected."

2. BLOCKING (0:20–0:40)
   Switch to Instagram. Open a DM, scroll photos (work normally).
   Open Reels: let it fail to load for ~5 s.
   Caption: "Messages and photos work. Reels do not load."

3. BREAK (0:40–0:55)
   Back in InstaBlock. Hold the "1 minute" card until the ring fills (5 s).
   Countdown appears. Switch to Instagram: a Reel plays.
   Caption: "Hold a card for 5 seconds to allow videos for a set time."

4. RE-BLOCK (0:55–1:20)
   Stay in Instagram until the minute ends: "Time's up" notification appears, Reels stall.
   Switch to InstaBlock: blocked state restored, cards locked "NEXT BREAK IN 0:05".
   Caption: "Videos block again automatically when the break ends."

5. OTHER VPN (optional, 1:20–1:35)
   Settings > VPN: enable another VPN. Notification "InstaBlock is off" with
   "Turn InstaBlock back on" action. Disable the other VPN, tap the action,
   InstaBlock shows blocked again.
   Caption: "Pauses for other VPNs; never disconnects them by itself."

Demo video (InstaBlock 1.0, recorded on a physical iPhone running iOS 26): <link>
The video shows the first-run explainer, the notification and VPN configuration permission
prompts, the blocked state, Instagram DMs and photos working while Reels fail to load, a timed
break started by holding a duration card, videos playing during the break, the automatic
re-block and "Time's up" notification when the break ends, and the app pausing when another VPN
is enabled and resuming from the notification action. There are no accounts, logins, purchases
or additional features. The video is valid for all storefronts.
