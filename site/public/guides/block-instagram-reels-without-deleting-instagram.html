How to block Instagram Reels without deleting Instagram (iPhone)

Yes — you can block Instagram Reels on an iPhone without deleting Instagram or losing your messages. iOS has no built-in setting for it, and Instagram doesn't offer one either, but a local network filter like InstaBlock can stop just the video servers while everything else in the app keeps working.

Why there's no setting for this

Instagram has never shipped an option to disable Reels; the closest it offers is a per-video "Not interested" and a daily time reminder you can dismiss. iOS Screen Time works at the app level — it can limit or block Instagram, but it has no idea what a Reel is. That leaves most people choosing between deleting the app (and losing DMs, group chats and stories) or white-knuckling it.

What actually works: block the video, not the app

Instagram loads photos and messages from its own servers, and streams video from content delivery hosts. A filter running on your phone can slow those video hosts to a trickle. Photos are small enough to get through; a video needs sustained bandwidth and simply never starts. From inside Instagram it looks like Reels are "broken" — the tab opens, the spinner turns, nothing plays — while the feed, DMs, stories and comments behave normally.

That is exactly what InstaBlock does:

  1. Install InstaBlock from the App Store and open it once. iOS asks to add a VPN configuration — tap Allow. (It's a local filter; nothing is routed through a server.)
  2. Blocking is now on, permanently. Force-quit Instagram and reopen it: Reels stop loading, DMs work.
  3. When you genuinely want to watch something, tap 1, 5, 15 or 30 minutes. Reels come back for that long and then stop again on their own — you don't have to remember to re-enable anything.

What to expect

  • Reels and video posts stall instead of playing; Instagram doesn't crash.
  • Photos may load slightly slower than usual. Messages are unaffected.
  • A VPN badge appears in the status bar while the filter is on.
  • Videos Instagram already downloaded (for example during a break) may still play once from cache.

Other options, honestly

  • Screen Time app limit: blocks all of Instagram; one tap on "Ignore Limit" defeats it.
  • Delete the app, use the website: instagram.com in Safari has a much weaker Reels experience, but DMs are clunky and you lose notifications.
  • Log out between sessions: adds friction, changes nothing about the feed once you're in.

Questions

Can I block Reels but keep Instagram messages?

Yes. A network filter that limits only Instagram's video servers, such as InstaBlock, stops Reels from playing while DMs, stories and photos keep working.

Does Instagram have a setting to turn off Reels?

No. Instagram offers 'Not interested' on individual videos and a dismissible daily time reminder, but no way to disable Reels.

Can Screen Time block only Reels?

No. Screen Time limits apps, not features inside an app; it can only block all of Instagram, and the limit can be ignored with one tap.
