How to turn off Reels on iPhone — and why Screen Time doesn't work

You can't turn off Reels inside Instagram or in iOS settings — no such switch exists. What you can do on iPhone is stop Reels from loading with an on-device network filter, which keeps the rest of Instagram working. Screen Time is not that: it only blocks the whole app, and its limit can be bypassed with one tap.

The approach that works: make Reels fail to load

Instagram streams video from dedicated media servers. If your phone limits traffic to just those servers, Reels can't buffer — but photos, messages and stories, which are tiny by comparison, still get through. That's a feature-level block that iOS can't do natively but a small on-device filter can.

InstaBlock is built around exactly this. Install it, allow the VPN configuration it asks for (it has no server — the "VPN" is just how iOS lets an app filter traffic locally), and Reels stop playing from then on. When you want to watch, tap a 1, 5, 15 or 30-minute break; the block returns by itself when the time is up, without opening the app.

Why "no off switch" is the point

Every Reels workaround dies the same way: a late-night tap on whatever undoes it. InstaBlock deliberately has no permanent off. You can take a timed break, and that's it — the only way to remove the block is to delete the app. Friction where it matters, none where it doesn't.

Questions

Is there a setting to turn off Reels in Instagram?

No. Instagram has no option to disable Reels on iPhone or Android.

Can iOS Screen Time turn off Reels?

No. Screen Time works at the app level and can't distinguish Reels from the rest of Instagram.

How does InstaBlock turn off Reels?

It runs an on-device network filter that limits bandwidth to Instagram's video servers, so Reels fail to load while messages, stories and photos keep working.
